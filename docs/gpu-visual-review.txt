GPU Rig overhaul and refinement
===============================
Shared stylized model: longer 4.5 x 2.48 chassis, top near 2.32, three recessed
seven-blade animated fans, layered metallic rims and cyan emissive rings.
Chamfered supports, vented top modules, fasteners and side sockets. Geometry
is cached/batched. No new point lights, bloom, physics or dependencies.

Founder HQ levels 1 and 2 have an exterior power box with a cyan bolt.
Per-rig cables use actual transformed ports and grounded rounded routes,
avoid buildings/walls when feasible, and update during placement/rotation.
Crowded legacy layouts can use buried conduit rather than moving structures.
Visual width fits neighboring obstacles while saved positions and logical
placement footprints remain unchanged. Geometry is disposed on rebuild.

Shared across HQ, visits, scouting/combat, title diorama and building images.
Destroyed PvP rig cables hide only within the battle instance. Production,
storage, collection, balance, saves, recovery, accounts and DB are unchanged.
