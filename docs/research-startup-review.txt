THE AI RACE — RESEARCH V1 / CAMPUS STARTUP RELEASE

Scope
- Three permanent technologies, three levels each, with total baseline bonuses of 10%, 20%, 30%.
- GPU Optimization affects both rigs' server accrual, offline production, collection and displayed production.
- Cipher Bot Engineering snapshots damage for new infiltrations; actual drone shots and wall breaches use it.
- Bot Training Efficiency gives newly ordered bots 4.5s / 4s / 3.5s sequential jobs. Existing queued deadlines remain unchanged.
- Each GPU Rig independently stores 250 uncollected Compute. Company Compute capacity stays 1,000.

Economy
Research Lab remains 2 Research/minute, storing 20, with company Research capacity 500.
Costs remain the requested 50 / 125 / 250 for each technology. Without infiltration income those take 25 / 62.5 / 125 minutes of production per level. Frequent collection is still needed.

Persistence / compatibility
The existing version-1 JSON save format accepts additive optional technologies and trainingDurations fields. Missing technology levels read as zero without rewriting or replacing old saves. Valid existing technology levels are retained.
Purchase uses the existing identity-checked BEGIN IMMEDIATE transaction and action receipts: accrue at the prior production rate, validate completed lab / expected level / balance / maximum, debit and save together. Request IDs make retries idempotent. Distinct concurrent requests with a stale expected level cannot buy the next level inadvertently.
Existing company identity, recovery, buildings, troops, queue deadlines, mission state, resources and Influence are preserved. No production database reset or schema replacement.
Existing missions retain their original damage snapshot; new missions use the purchased level.

Ascent reference (read only)
Inspected current src/AscentSplash.tsx, src/ascent-splash.css and src/App.tsx in Documents/Ascent (base commit 996258f). Reference timing is 2,650ms linear progress plus 350ms fade. No Ascent files were edited by this task.
The AI Race keeps its Master Studios splash, existing miniature campus, muted preferences and soundtrack. Campus power-on uses a GPU pulse, short Builder Bot walk, lab lights and one traveling energy pulse. Progress is capped at 94% until saved state, HQ scene and required modules are ready. Recovery/session errors pause entry; no automatic replacement company. Reduced motion uses the powered scene and removes the fade/stripe motion.
